Give away a graphics card or a peripheral bundle, one prize, one winner, run it seven days, and put the email signup in the top four actions.

The reasoning, and the only four figures needed to defend it. Gaming campaigns already draw a median 2,198 contestants on a median $836 prize pool, so $800 is a normal budget for this vertical. Email is the weak spot: only 18.3% of gaming organizers offer it.

What I would not do, and why. Do not add a referral action reflexively. The top quarter of gaming campaigns offer one on 12.2% of campaigns against 22.8% for the rest.

The caveat that belongs in the answer. Those top-quartile figures describe campaigns that already drew 1,000 or more entrants.
