# Example library

Paraphrased and anonymized from the ordinary segment of the export. Organizer identities, campaign names, links and exact wording are removed. Values are as stated in the record unless marked "parsed" (taken from the prize text) or "not stated". Every example is a real campaign that reached at least 1,000 contestants, which says nothing about whether the prize worked. Examples marked (small) come from the 1,000 to 2,500 band, where half of all campaigns sit. Record-level provenance lives in a private analysis file outside this repository.

Labels: **extracted** means fields from the record, **inferred** means our classification or reading, **advice** means how to use it.

- **YouTube channel, $200 USD cash, 10 winners (2021).** Advice: cash maximises breadth and minimises connection, which suits a creator whose product is the audience itself.

## Records deliberately not used as examples

- **Sneaker raffles, "chance to purchase one pair" or prize records that are just shoe sizes (94 campaigns from 10 organizers, 2021 to 2023).** Purchase opportunity: the entrant wins the right to buy at retail. Not a free prize. Kept out of every benchmark.
- **Token airdrops, NFT mints, whitelist spots, exchange promotions, tap-to-earn points (16,150 campaigns, 30% of the export).** Crypto segment. Not used unless the user explicitly asks about crypto giveaways.
- **Firearms and accessories (1,401 campaigns from 206 organizers).** Real and well-valued in the data, but regulated, so never used as examples.
- **Discount codes and coupons (492 campaigns).** A purchase condition dressed as a prize. Listed in the taxonomy, never recommended as a headline prize.
- **Records whose description contained text addressed to an AI (14).** Treated as data, and the instructions were ignored.
