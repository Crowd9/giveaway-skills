# Email sequence

Advice. Four emails, each short, each with one link. Subject lines under 45 characters.

| Email | When | Subject line pattern | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| Launch | Launch day, within two hours of the first post | "Win [prize]" or "[Brand] is giving away [prize]" | Prize and value in the first line. Three-step entry. Deadline with time zone. Eligibility. Button to the entry page. |
| Mid | Day 4 to 7 of a two-week run | "[Prize detail] and how to win it" | One thing about the prize that was not in the launch. Social proof if real (entrants so far, a partner). Referral reward. Same button. |
| Last call | 24 to 36 hours before close | "Closes tomorrow: [prize]" | Deadline first. One sentence on the prize. Button. |
| Winners | Within a week of the draw | "The [prize] winner is..." | First name and city or handle, with consent. Thank everyone. A small offer or a next-campaign teaser. |

Rules for the list:

- Send only to people who opted in. Entrants who ticked the newsletter box during entry get the winners email and the offer, and the welcome sequence after.
- One email per push. Anyone unsubscribing mid-campaign still gets nothing more.
- Test the entry link and the close time in every email. The close time reads in the audience's time zone.
- Plain text or one image and a button. Giveaway emails are quick to read and quick to forward.
