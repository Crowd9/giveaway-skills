# Timeline template

Advice. Fill in the dates and delete what does not apply.

## Two-week campaign

| When | What |
|---|---|
| T minus 10 days | Terms written (eligibility, dates with time zone, prize value, how winners are chosen and contacted, redraw rule). Prize secured or ordered. Partner briefs sent. |
| T minus 5 days | Entry page built and tested on a phone. Assets for every channel ready. Email announcement drafted. |
| T minus 1 day | Teaser post. Team knows who answers questions and who draws. |
| Launch day (a weekday) | Announce on every channel within the same two hours. Email the list. Pin the post. |
| Day 3 and day 7 | Push two: show the prize in use, share early entrant count if it helps, remind of the sharing action. |
| Day 10 to 12 | Push three: partner posts, a story countdown. |
| Final 48 hours | "Closing soon" on every channel. Check the close time reads correctly in the audience's time zone. |
| Close plus 1 day | Draw. Verify the winning entry. Contact winners with a 72-hour reply deadline. |
| Close plus 4 days | Redraw unclaimed prizes. Announce winners publicly. Thank entrants and offer a small consolation (a code, a content link) if the objective was leads. |
| Close plus 7 to 21 days | Ship or deliver. Ask winners for a photo or review if the terms allowed it. |

## Adjusting the length

- One week: a single push, a small list, one channel. Fewer entries per contestant because repeatable actions have less time.
- Three to four weeks: a content series, two or more partners, or a product launch with staged reveals.
- Longer than six weeks: only with daily repeatable actions, fresh content each week, and a reason the audience keeps coming back. Otherwise the middle goes quiet.

## Seasonal notes

- December is the busiest month for launches in the data, and December starts also ran 15% more contestants and converted 23% better in the clean comparison. More competition, more attention, slower shipping. Close before the shipping cut-off if the prize must arrive for a holiday.
- Late January and February are quiet and cheap for attention.
- Align with the audience's calendar, not the business's. A back-to-school prize in the week schools return, a gardening prize in early spring for that hemisphere.
- Avoid closing on a public holiday or a weekend when nobody on the team will draw and respond.
