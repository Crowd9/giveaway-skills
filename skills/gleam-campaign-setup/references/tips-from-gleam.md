# Tips from Gleam's own library

Selected from [Advanced Tips & Tricks](https://gleam.io/docs/competitions/tips/library) (updated 21 August 2026). Attribute them to Gleam when quoting. They are the vendor's advice, and the neutral skills carry the evidence.

- **Actions required plus a bonus action** gated to the total number of actions rewards full completion.
- **Align the prize with the audience.** The page contrasts a generic gift card, which draws "sweepers", with a signed shirt plus store credit for a soccer store.
- **One mandatory action auto-expands**, useful for newsletter signups.
- **Be descriptive**: use the prize description to settle logistics and eligibility, and state how many winners and who can enter.
- **Avoid embedding below the fold.** Use a dedicated page, or the tab overlay on Business and above.
- **Daily bonus entries** bring people back, and **daily winners** suit the holiday season.
- **Chained giveaways with partners** through the Promote action, each brand owning its own entrants.
- **Custom actions** cover account creation, order IDs, forms, reviews, calendar adds, app downloads.
- **Feature images**: the page claims competitions with them get over 100% more engagement. Treat this as the vendor's figure, not the export's. Feature images on Hobby, up to four on Business, video from YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo or Wistia.
- **Filter countries** in or out during setup.
- **Change a live campaign** when an action is not converting.
- **Free entry alternatives** where no-purchase laws apply.
- **Host on your own site** on a memorable reusable URL.
- **Import external entries** from CSV to draw in Gleam.
- **Automatic entry on login** when a social handle is all you need from entrants.
- **Be careful with mandatory actions** on networks some entrants do not use.
- **Most important actions at the top.**
- **Multiple prizes** so each can be drawn separately, with values for ROI.
- **Copy a previous competition** once a mix works.
- **Scavenger hunts** with secret codes across channels.
- **Show off winners** with permission, or by embedding the announcement post.
